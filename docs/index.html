Bott Monument has served families across the Rocky Mountain region for over three decades, crafting custom memorials in granite, marble, and bronze that stand for generations. Founded in Wyoming and proudly family-owned, we are not a factory — we are a studio guided by craftsmanship, driven by care, and committed to the kind of personal service that only a family business can offer.
